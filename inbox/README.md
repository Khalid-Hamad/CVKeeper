# Put your existing CV here

Drop a copy of your CV into this folder, then open CVKeeper in your AI assistant.
PDF, Word, or plain-text CVs can be used if your assistant can read them.

Tell the assistant what you want:

> Read AGENTS.md and the CV in inbox/. Be my CV writer and updater. Build my
> private career profile from it, then ask about important experience, results,
> evidence, and timeline overlaps that are missing or unclear. Preserve my
> facts and original file.

You can also ask to import the CV with its wording unchanged. A full interview
is not needed when the information is already in your document. For scanned
PDFs, you may need to confirm extracted text or paste it into the conversation.

Only this guide is part of the public starter. Other files placed here are
ignored by Git and excluded by the starter ZIP packager. Do not edit this
guide to include personal details. Keep a private backup of your original CV.
