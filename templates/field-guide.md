## Evidence to ask for

Adapt the prompts in guides/FIELD-PRACTICES.md to this career. Do not treat
recommended evidence as a fact about the person.

## Fit to target jobs

Map confirmed experience to relevant requirements. Keep unsupported requirements
as gaps; never add them as skills merely because a job asks for them.

## Source notes and limitations

Store exact URLs and dates for sources actually read. If browsing is unavailable,
say so and label field advice provisional. Revisit when the target changes.
